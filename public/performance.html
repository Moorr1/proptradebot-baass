Headline edge and every calendar figure are realised points per contract — what the trade actually closed for, taken straight from the pts field of the published ledger, so you can sum the file yourself and get the same number.

Realised means realised on the ladder the signal published: first target 7 points, second target 10 points, half the position at each, structural stop. Those targets are printed in the alert itself, before the trade fills, so the figure is reproducible rather than a setting we chose after the fact. Run different targets and you will get a different number — that is your deviation from the instruction, not a gap in the record.

Maximum favourable excursion is shown separately as “offered”: what the move was worth at its best tick if you had held for it. It is context for anyone considering a different ladder. It is never added into the edge, because nobody exits at the high.

Position sizing is yours, we publish points so the figures do not depend on a contract count you did not choose.
