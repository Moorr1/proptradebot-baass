Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Information We Collect

PropTradeBot collects the following information:

  • Account information: Email address, name, and billing information when you subscribe.
  • API usage: Trading signals processed, bot configuration, and performance metrics.
  • Bot status: Periodic heartbeat and trade counts sent by your bot so we can confirm your subscription is active and show accurate figures on your dashboard.
  • Waitlist: Your email address and, if you give it, your Discord or Telegram handle. We use them only to send your invite and PropTradeBot updates. Ask us to remove them at any time.
  • Setup assistant: The messages you send the dashboard's setup assistant, kept for 30 days. Keys and passwords are removed before a message is stored or sent to the AI provider.
  • Site visits and downloads: When a page or the app download is requested, our server records the time, which page or version, the website that linked to it, and whether the request looks automated. No cookies, no tracking scripts, and no IP address or browser details are stored, so these counts can't identify you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the PropTradeBot service
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send important service updates and alerts
  • Improve our product and troubleshoot issues

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use Clerk for authentication and Whop (and, for older accounts, Stripe) for payment processing. Your full payment details are never stored on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Whop — Payment processing and memberships
  • Stripe — Payment processing (older accounts)
  • Clerk — Authentication
  • Render — Hosting
  • Neon — Database
  • Anthropic — AI model behind the setup assistant (receives your assistant messages with keys and passwords removed)

5. Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at support@proptradebot.com.
