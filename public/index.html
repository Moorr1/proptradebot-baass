The part that protects your accounts

It refuses rather than guesses.

Only TradingView alerts in PropTradeBot's format, and Discord messages matching what you taught it, are traded. Anything else is refused, with the reason shown in the app.

Bring your own strategy

Two ways in. The same managed trade out.

Pick your firm in the guided setup and PropTradeBot configures the connection: no endpoint URLs, no config files. Set your sizing, trims, stop and accounts once, and save it as your preset.

TradingView

  1. Copy your webhook and alert message from your PropTradeBot dashboard.
  2. Paste both into a TradingView alert on your strategy.
  3. Every alert runs your preset on every account you chose.

Discord provider

  1. Install the PropTradeBot Connector in Chrome and keep your provider's channel open.
  2. Teach it their format by pasting one real entry and exit message.
  3. Watch it in shadow mode SHADOW, then switch to live.
