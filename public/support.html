Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install the Chrome extension?

Download the extension from your dashboard, unzip it, then go to chrome://extensions/, enable Developer mode, and click "Load unpacked".

The bot says "Not connected" — what's wrong?

Make sure PropTradeBot is running on your Mac and the server URL in the extension matches (usually http://localhost:5555).

Can I use PropTradeBot on Windows?

Currently PropTradeBot is macOS-only. Windows support is on our roadmap.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from your dashboard. Your access continues until the end of your billing period.

Is my trading data secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption and never store your broker credentials. All API keys are encrypted at rest.
