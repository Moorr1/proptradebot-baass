Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a browser extension?

No. Signals are delivered from our servers directly to your bot, which checks for them every few seconds. Nothing needs to be open in a browser. Earlier versions used a Chrome extension that read signals off the Discord page — that is no longer used or required, and you can remove it if you installed it.

The bot says "Not connected" — what's wrong?

Check that PropTradeBot is running on your Mac, then look at its Status and Connection tabs. If it says your API key was rejected, generate a fresh key on your dashboard, then in PropTradeBot open Connection → Run setup again and paste it.

Can I use PropTradeBot on Windows?

Currently PropTradeBot is macOS-only. Windows support is on our roadmap.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Email support@proptradebot.com and we'll cancel immediately. Your access continues until the end of your billing period. No questions asked.

Is my trading data secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption and never store your broker credentials. All API keys are encrypted at rest. Your trading bot runs locally on your Mac — signals and orders never pass through our servers.

What signal formats are supported?

TradingView alerts, in PropTradeBot's alert format: the app gives you the exact message to paste, with a Copy button. The chart must be the contract you trade. Anything else is refused, with the reason shown in the app; PropTradeBot never guesses. Discord signal support is in development.

What happens when the market is closed?

The bot stays connected and ready. If a signal arrives while the market is closed, the order will be rejected by your broker — no risk of unintended fills. The bot logs the rejection and continues monitoring.

Will I miss trades if my Mac goes to sleep?

Yes. PropTradeBot runs locally on your Mac and must be awake to receive signals and execute trades. We recommend disabling sleep mode during trading hours (System Settings → Battery → Prevent automatic sleeping). The bot will reconnect automatically when your Mac wakes up.

Which prop firms are supported?

Today PropTradeBot supports Topstep (through the TopstepX / ProjectX API). We add a firm only when it confirms in writing that its accounts can get API access and that automated trading is allowed. See Topstep's automation rules below. Don't see your firm? Let us know at support@proptradebot.com.
