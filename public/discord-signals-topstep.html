How to auto-trade Discord signals on Topstep

PropTradeBot reads your signal provider's Discord channel in Chrome and turns each entry and exit call into orders on your Topstep accounts. Every trade gets a stop, your trims and a runner, and it's flat by your end-of-day time. It runs on Mac and Windows, and you teach it your provider's message format once.

Why I built it

The calls kept landing while I was at work. More on that here.

How it works

  1. Install the PropTradeBot Connector for Chrome. It reads the Discord channel you have open. Nothing else.
  2. Teach the app your provider's format. Paste one real entry message (like ES Long 7712) and one exit message (like all out). From then on it trades only messages that match. A message that could mean two things is never traded.
  3. Start in Shadow. The app logs what it would have traded without placing anything. When the log looks right, switch the provider to Live.
  4. Keep Chrome open on the channel while you trade, and keep the app running.

Each call becomes one market entry with a stop on every position, then T1 and T2 trims and a runner, using your own sizes, targets and stops. One signal can trade several Topstep accounts, directly or through Topstep's Trade Copier.

What it won't do

  • Guess. A message it doesn't understand is not traded, and the reason shows in the app.
  • Chase. An entry is refused when the market is more than 10 points (S&P) or 40 points (Nasdaq) from the provider's price, or when it can't read a current price. That catches typos and late messages.
  • Stack trades. A second entry while a trade is open is refused.
  • Hold past your flatten time. You set it; after it, no new entries.

What you need

  • A Mac that stays awake during your session, with Google Chrome.
  • A Topstep account with API access. Topstep bills that separately; see Topstep's API access page for the current price and how to get your key.
  • Your provider on Discord. Telegram is not supported.
